# Store listing copy (draft)

**Name:** Darkr

**Summary (132 characters max):**
A calm, local-first reading theme for the web. No account, tracking, or network requests.

**Description**

Darkr restyles the pages you read into Dark, Sepia, Grayscale or Warm Light —
entirely on your device.

- One switch turns Darkr on or off for the site you're on.
- Choose whether a change applies to this site or to all sites.
- Dark mode with a continuous background tone (true black to light grey) and a contrast control.
- Leaves sites that already have a dark appearance alone, unless you choose otherwise.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+Shift+D (this site), Alt+Shift+P (pause everywhere).
- Export and import your settings as a file.

**Privacy**

Darkr makes no network requests. It collects no analytics, accounts, page text
or browsing history. Settings stay in local extension storage. Host access to
http/https pages is used only to read computed colours and apply a visual theme.

**Single purpose:** change the colours of web pages for comfortable reading.

**Permission justifications**
- `storage`: save your preferences locally.
- `scripting`: load Darkr into tabs that were already open when the extension is installed or updated, so they are themed without a reload.
- Host access (`http://*/*`, `https://*/*`): read computed colours and apply the theme on pages you visit.
