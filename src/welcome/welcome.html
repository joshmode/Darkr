Welcome to Darkr

Darkr restyles the pages you read into Dark, Sepia, Grayscale or Warm Light — entirely on your device.

Getting started

  1. Pin the icon. Open the puzzle-piece menu in your toolbar and pin Darkr so the switch is one click away.
  2. Reload open tabs. Pages that were open before you installed Darkr need a reload before they can be themed.
  3. Use the switch. The popup turns Darkr on or off for the site you're on, and changes appearance for this site or all sites.
  4. Shortcuts. Alt+Shift+D toggles the current site, Alt+Shift+P pauses everywhere. You can change them in your browser's extension shortcuts page.

Private by design

Darkr makes no network requests and collects nothing: no analytics, accounts, page text or browsing history. Your settings stay in local extension storage.
