Per-site preferences

Site rules

Domains are matched exactly. A setting for example.com does not automatically apply to docs.example.com unless you tick Include subdomains on that rule. The www. alias is treated as the same site when it leaves a valid hostname.

“Always” themes a listed site even while Darkr is paused. “Never” leaves it untouched. Sites without a rule are “Automatic”, which follows the setting below.
